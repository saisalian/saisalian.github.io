Hi, I'm Sai. I'm a security engineer based in Dublin, working mostly with the Microsoft security stack and increasingly on the risk and governance side of security.

## What I do

I'm a hands-on security engineer: identity and access, endpoint protection, detection and investigation, and data protection. My specialism is the Microsoft security ecosystem (Entra ID, Defender, Intune, Sentinel, Purview, KQL and PowerShell). A growing part of my work is turning technical findings into risk decisions people outside IT can act on.

## Background

Before my current role I worked at a managed service provider, looking after security for more than 70 Microsoft 365 tenants at once. That taught me to spot patterns quickly, to standardise what can be standardised, and to explain risk to people who don't live in security tooling.

- **MSc in Cybersecurity**, National College of Ireland
- **BEng in Electronics Engineering**, University of Mumbai
- **Certifications:** Microsoft SC-200 (Security Operations Analyst), CompTIA Security+

## What I write about

- **Microsoft 365 security** in practice: configurations, detections, KQL, and the trade-offs behind them
- **Risk and governance**: risk registers, policies, audits, and making security measurable
- **Copilot and AI governance**: getting data access and labelling right before switching AI on

I write for people doing the same kind of work, especially anyone who is the only security person in the room.

The easiest way to reach me is through [GitHub](https://github.com/saisalian). I'm always happy to hear from people working on similar problems.
